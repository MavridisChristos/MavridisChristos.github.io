I am an Assistant Professor at TU Wien Informatics, where I lead the LearnCPS lab. We bring together learning, optimization, and control theory to study and develop intelligent and interacting Cyber-Physical-Human Systems.

Before joining TU Wien in 2026, I was a postdoctoral associate at KTH Royal Institute of Technology with Prof. Karl Henrik Johansson, and PI of a 2-year SSF project in collaboration with Ericsson Research. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Maryland, College Park, with Prof. John S. Baras, and my Diploma in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the National Technical University of Athens with Prof. Kostas J. Kyriakopoulos.
